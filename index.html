Privacy Policy

Chess 3D by Pixeloard

Effective date: 15 October 2026

1. Introduction

Pixeloard respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Chess 3D, an offline chess game for Android.

2. Accounts and Personal Information

Chess 3D does not require you to create an account or sign in. The game does not ask you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, or other personal details to play.

3. Offline Gameplay and Data Storage

Chess 3D is designed to support offline gameplay. Game settings and progress may be stored locally on your device, depending on the features you use. Local data may be removed if you clear the application's data or uninstall the game.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The current release does not include advertising or advertising SDKs. Pixeloard does not intentionally collect analytics through the game. If future versions introduce advertising, analytics, or other data-processing services, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

5. Device Permissions and Third-Party Services

The game is intended to work without an online account. Android, Google Play, or the device manufacturer may process technical information under their own privacy terms. Any third-party component included in the game may also have its own data practices.

6. Children's Privacy

Chess 3D is a chess game intended for general audiences. We do not intentionally request personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of mobile applications.

7. Data Security and Retention

Game data stored locally remains subject to the security and storage controls of your device. You can generally remove local application data through Android settings or uninstall the game.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game changes or when necessary to reflect legal or privacy requirements. The latest version will be available at this webpage.
